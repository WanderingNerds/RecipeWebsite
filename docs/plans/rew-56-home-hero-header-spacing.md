## Summary
Two independent, presentation-only CSS issues on/near the home page, both traced to specific rules in `public/css/styles.css`. First, the hero section (`.hero` in `views/home.ejs`) is oversized relative to its content: `min-height: 38rem` (570px at the site's 15px root font size) combined with `display: grid; place-items: center` and generous `padding: clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem) 1rem` (75–135px top and bottom) forces the browser to insert extra centering space around the h1/lede/button block whenever that content is shorter than the min-height, and that slack is most visually obvious directly above the `.hero-wave` divider since the olive background is otherwise flat and featureless there. Second, in `views/partials/navbar.ejs`, three separate small `gap` values compound to make the header row feel cramped: the nav links (`.navbar-nav`) use `gap: 1.4rem` (21px) for up to 5 links plus the user/logout block, the search input and Search button (`.search-form-nav`, inheriting from `.search-form`) use `gap: var(--space-2)` (8.8px), and the "Hello, {name}" / Logout pairing (`.navbar-user`) uses `gap: var(--space-3)` (13.2px). This plan reduces the hero's forced height/padding and increases the three header gaps to give both areas comfortable, consistent breathing room, with no HTML structure changes.
